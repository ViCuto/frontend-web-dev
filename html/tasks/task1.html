How to make pancakes:

  1. Mix flour, eggs, and milk to make the batter.
  2. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the batter into the pan and spread it evenly.
  4. Cook until bubbles form, then flip it carefully.
  5. Cook until golden brown and serve.
